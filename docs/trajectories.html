How to read these records

Main-experiment rows preserve all 3,316 planned attacker/world outcomes, including provider filtering and unresolved cases. A metadata-only row does not imply a complete interaction was saved. Attack success is the original task-defined realized deviation, not necessarily a monetary loss.

The 976 companion-study records use frozen saved inputs or fixed authorization pairs. Their outcomes are labelled deviations rather than new attacker-model successes. Refusals, escalations and pending results remain separate. No additional model judge is used here.

Detail files are publication derivatives of saved episodes and requests. Transport headers, account metadata, local paths, credential patterns and private model-thinking blocks are not published. Original source-file hashes and disclosure notes identify the retained evidence.
