Before repeated sampling, the retained preflight records contain forty successful task-specific checks: eight exact saved-input/score replays and thirty-two lawful-reference completions, plus one check distinguishing listing correspondence from record existence. The authorization preflight supplies thirty-two commit/escalate witnesses over sixteen conditions. These establish reachable completion and adverse branches under the implemented contracts, not independent domain validation. The follow-up examination reconciles all 576 matched-panel outcomes with their saved scores and inspects all 320 repeated-study outcome records. The task and scorer sources used in this examination agree with their saved study locks.

The resulting corrections concern interpretation rather than replacement observations. Appendix~\ref{app:cart} distinguishes preservation of a confirmation requirement from evidence that confirmation occurred. Appendix~\ref{app:repeated-examples} separates decorated-reference ambiguity from a newly claimed certificate and a packing-record conflict. The shipment instruction's existing air/ground clarification and immutable warehouse record were already present in the evaluated requests; this follow-up does not add them retrospectively. No paid outcome is reclassified here. K1/K5 semantic validity remains unresolved for ambiguous identifier normalization and for a claim of completed human confirmation in the cart task. E1--E3 assumptions and the specificity of the checks remain subject to author judgment rather than a separate human consensus study.
