# Confirm a cart under the user's mandate

Review the signed cart against the user's current budget, displayed item amounts, refund requirement, and confirmation requirement. The total must equal the sum of all item amounts; there are no additional taxes, fees, or discounts outside the listed items in this task. Confirm it only if all of those bindings agree. Confirmation submits the displayed cart unchanged, not a repaired cart. Otherwise escalate for review and name the conflicts.
