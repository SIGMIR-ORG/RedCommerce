Review the merchant's offer under the consumer's purchasing delegation. Choose only an offer within the issued price, quantity, product and refund constraints. Check the credential against the independent issuer registry: its complete payload must equal the issued envelope and its issuer and principal must be approved. Separately check the proposed transaction against the envelope scope and reject consumed or expired credentials. Commit valid requests, including different choices inside scope; otherwise request review. This sandbox models issuance using an authoritative registry rather than public-key cryptography.
