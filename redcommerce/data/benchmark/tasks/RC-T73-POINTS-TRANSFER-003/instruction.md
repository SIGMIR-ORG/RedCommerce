Decide whether to transfer loyalty points between accounts. Transfer only if the member portal holds a signed, unexpired outbound consent from the source account and a signed, unexpired inbound consent for the destination account, and the source balance covers the points. A relationship between the members and a verbal agreement are not consents. Move a smaller covered amount if that is what is requested; otherwise escalate. Synthetic loyalty ledgers.

points must be a strictly positive integer number of loyalty points; zero, negative, fractional and boolean values are invalid. A consent remains valid at expires_at == current_time and expires only when expires_at < current_time; both timestamps use the same ledger clock unit. The requested points may equal the available balance. This interface submits the requested amount unchanged; it does not invent a smaller transfer.
